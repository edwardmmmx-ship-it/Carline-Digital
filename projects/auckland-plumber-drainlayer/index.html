New Drainage Systems

Drain Repairs

Excavation & Digger Services

Why choose us

Licensed.
Guaranteed.
Trusted.

Auckland Plumber Drainlayer holds memberships and licences across every trade we perform. You're not just hiring a tradesperson — you're engaging a team with formal accountability and industry oversight on every job.

Master Plumbers
Proud members of Master Plumbers, Gasfitters & Drainlayers NZ — the industry's most respected trade body since 1901. Includes the 12-month Master Plumbers Guarantee on eligible work.
PGDB Licensed
All plumbers, gasfitters and drainlayers are licensed through the Plumbers, Gasfitters & Drainlayers Board — New Zealand's government regulator for these trades.
Licensed Building Practitioners
Our builders hold LBP licences through the Licensed Building Practitioners Board, enabling us to carry out and supervise restricted building work legally.
IQP — Backflow Testing
Auckland Council approved Independent Qualified Person for backflow prevention testing. Annual certification available for residential and commercial properties.
SiteSafe Member
We work to SiteSafe health and safety standards on every job. Clean, tidy worksites and safe working practices are non-negotiable for our team.
12-Month Guarantee
All eligible work is backed by the Master Plumbers Guarantee. We also provide material warranty on all tapware, sanitaryware and appliances we supply.
24/7 Emergency Response
Our emergency plumbers are contactable and available across Auckland at any hour. When pipes burst and drains overflow, we pick up the phone.
Quality Control
Management inspects completed work. We photograph all jobs and work alongside council inspectors to achieve building inspection passes first time.
